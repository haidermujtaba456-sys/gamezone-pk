About Pong vs AI

Pong vs AI recreates the very first video game duel in a clean modern browser version. You control the left paddle, the computer controls the right, and a ball bounces between you. Move your paddle up and down to return the ball, and try to send it past the computer paddle to score. The first player to reach seven points wins the match. The controls are simple — arrow keys or mouse movement — but the game is all about timing and angles. Hitting the ball with the edge of your paddle changes its angle, so you can aim for the corners and make the computer work harder. The AI tracks the ball and gets better as the rally continues, so long rallies become faster and more intense. Pong is a wonderful piece of gaming history and still a genuinely fun contest today. It teaches prediction, because you must watch the ball, judge its path, and move before it arrives. Play a quick match, enjoy the retro feel, and try to win by a clear margin.

Pong vs AI is gaming history you can play. It is simple, fast, and surprisingly competitive — a long rally against the computer is as tense as any modern match. Players return because the game is honest: the better player wins, and the better player can be you.

How to Play

Move your paddle with the arrow keys or mouse. Return the ball and score past the AI. First to 7 points wins.
