About Neon Flap

Neon Flap is a simple one-button arcade game with a famously hard challenge. Your glowing bird flies forward on its own, and your only job is to tap, click, or press space to flap its wings and stay in the air. Neon pipes appear in your path with gaps you must fly through. Miss the gap or touch a pipe and the flight ends. The controls could not be simpler, but the timing is everything: flap too often and you rise too high, flap too rarely and you fall. The best players find a steady rhythm, make small corrections, and stay calm as the pipes come faster. Each pipe you pass adds a point, and the game keeps your best score so you always have a target to beat. It is the ideal game for short breaks and friendly competition, because a round lasts seconds or minutes and anyone can learn it instantly. There are no levels to unlock and no items to buy, only your reflexes and your patience. Try once, try again, and watch your score climb as your timing improves.

Neon Flap is famous for one thing: it is easy to learn and almost impossible to master. That gap is what makes it addictive. Every player believes the next attempt will be the good one, and sometimes it is. The game asks for nothing but your timing, and it gives back a score that is entirely your own.

How to Play

Click, tap, or press Space to flap. Fly through the gaps in the pipes. One touch ends the flight.

  • Mouse: Click buttons and move the paddle in Breakout and Pong.
