About Whack-a-Mole

Whack-a-Mole is a fast reflex game that anyone can play and everyone enjoys. Moles pop up from holes in a grid at random, stay visible for a short moment, and then disappear. Your job is to tap or click each mole before it vanishes. Every hit adds a point, and you have thirty seconds to score as many as you can. The game starts gently, then the moles appear faster and in less predictable places, so you must watch the whole grid instead of staring at one hole. Good players keep their eyes moving, tap with short quick movements, and stay relaxed so their reactions stay fast. It is a great game for children, for warming up your reflexes, and for quick friendly competitions, because a round is short and the rules are obvious. The score and best score are shown clearly, so you always know what to beat next. There is no complicated strategy, only speed, attention, and a little practice. Play one round, then play again and see how much faster your hands get.

Whack-a-Mole is pure fun in thirty seconds. There is no learning curve, no strategy to memorise, and no way to feel bad about a low score — you simply play again, faster. It is the game players choose when they want a quick laugh and a fast challenge.

How to Play

Tap each mole as soon as it appears. You have 30 seconds to score as many hits as you can.

Controls

  • Keyboard: Arrow keys to move or slide, Space to shoot or flap where the game supports it.
  • Mouse: Click buttons and move the paddle in Breakout and Pong.
  • Touch: On-screen buttons and tap controls work on phones and tablets.
