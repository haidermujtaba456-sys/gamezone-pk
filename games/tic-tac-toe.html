About Tic-Tac-Toe vs AI

Tic-Tac-Toe vs AI takes the simplest strategy game in the world and gives you a computer opponent to test yourself against. You play as X and the computer plays as O on a three-by-three grid. The first player to get three marks in a row, a column, or a diagonal wins. If the board fills with no winner, the game is a draw. The rules take seconds to learn, but the strategy is real: take the centre when you can, block the computer when it threatens to win, and set up two winning lines at once so your opponent can only block one. The AI in this version plays sensibly, so you must think ahead instead of placing marks at random. The game is perfect for children learning strategy, for quick breaks, and for anyone who wants a clean, fair contest with no luck involved. Every game is short, you can restart instantly, and the score tracker shows your wins, draws, and losses across the session. It is a reminder that even the simplest games reward planning, and that a careful player can turn a draw into a win.

Tic-Tac-Toe vs AI is the perfect quick game: it takes a minute, it is always fair, and it teaches real strategy. Players return because a draw can be turned into a win with one clever move, and because beating the computer, even in a simple game, is genuinely satisfying.
