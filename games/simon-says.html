About Simon Says

Simon Says is a memory and attention game built around colour and sound. Four coloured pads light up in a sequence, and you must repeat that sequence in the same order. Each round adds one more step, so the sequence grows longer and harder to remember. The game starts with a single colour and builds slowly, which makes it friendly for beginners, but after a few rounds it becomes a genuine memory challenge. The best way to play is to watch carefully, listen to the tones, and repeat the pattern out loud or in your head as it plays. Breaking a long sequence into small groups also helps. Simon Says is especially good for children and for anyone who wants to train focus and short-term memory in a playful way. There is no time pressure while you repeat the sequence, only the need to remember it correctly. Every round is a fresh test, and your score shows how many steps you remembered. Play regularly and you will notice your memory for sequences improving, one colour at a time.

Simon Says is a game that grows with you. The first rounds are easy, but the sequence keeps growing, and the moment you realise you remembered eight colours in a row is a genuine achievement. Players return to see how far their memory can go, and to beat their own best round.

How to Play

Watch the colours light up, then repeat the sequence in the same order. Each round adds one more step.
