Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. GameZone PK (gamezonepk.com) is operated from Pakistan.

This Privacy Policy explains what information GameZone PK collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. By using this website, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect very little information. When you use the contact form or subscribe to the newsletter on this demo version, your message is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and is not sent to our servers. When a real form backend is connected, the information you type — your name, email address, subject, and message — will be sent to us so we can reply. We do not ask for passwords, payment details, national ID numbers, or any sensitive personal information, and you should never send such information through the contact form.

2. Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar local storage for basic functions: remembering your recently played games, storing newsletter and contact-form entries on your device, and keeping the site working correctly. Third-party advertising and analytics providers, if enabled in future, may also use cookies to show relevant advertising and measure traffic. You can delete cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings.

3. Google AdSense Advertising

We intend to use Google AdSense to show advertisements on this website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices. Advertising is currently shown only as clearly labelled placeholder spaces; live AdSense advertisements will appear only after our account and site are approved.

4. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads on this site. Other advertising partners may be added in future, and this policy will be updated to list them. We do not control third-party cookies, and their privacy policies apply to their own data practices.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools in future to understand which pages and games are popular, so we can improve the site. Any analytics tool used will be listed here when it is enabled. We do not sell personal data to anyone.

6. Data Usage and Retention

Information you send us is used only to reply to you, improve the website, and, where required, comply with the law. We keep contact messages only as long as needed to handle your request. Information stored in your own browser stays on your device until you clear it.

7. Children's Privacy

Our games are family-friendly, but the website is not directed at children under 13 as a data-collection service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@gamezonepk.com. You may also disable cookies in your browser, although some site features may then work less well.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the site changes, for example when AdSense or analytics are enabled. The updated date at the top of this page will change, and the new policy will apply from that date.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, email hello@gamezonepk.com.
