How to Submit a Takedown Request

Send an email to hello@gamezonepk.com with the subject line "DMCA Takedown Request" and include all of the following:

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notification to the same email address, with the subject line "DMCA Counter-Notification", including: your full name, address, telephone number, and email; identification of the material removed and its former URL; a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification; a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the courts in Pakistan; and your physical or electronic signature.
