About GameZone PK

GameZone PK is a free gaming portal from Pakistan, built for players who want good games without downloads, accounts, or payments. We make original browser games, write honest gaming news and reviews, and publish practical guides that help players improve — all in plain language, and all free to use.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: make gaming accessible. Not everyone owns a powerful PC or a flagship phone, and not everyone wants to install a large game before knowing whether it is fun. A good browser game starts in seconds, works on almost any device, and respects the player's time, data, and privacy. That is the standard every game on this site is built to meet.

What We Offer

  • Free original browser games — 12 games at launch, across arcade, puzzle, shooting, strategy, sports, and kids categories.
  • Gaming news — original articles about the industry, PC, mobile, and console gaming.
  • Honest reviews — reviews of the games on this site, with clear criteria, pros and cons, and a verdict.
  • Practical guides — beginner basics, advanced strategy, performance optimisation, controls, and multiplayer advice.

Editorial Standards

All content on GameZone PK is original. We do not copy articles, reviews, or descriptions from other websites. We do not publish fake release dates, fake reviews of games we have not played, or misleading download buttons. When we express an opinion, we say so. When we do not know something, we say that too.

Content Creation Process

Every game is designed and coded for this website, with original art drawn in code. Every article is written by our editorial team, checked for accuracy, and reviewed before publication. Every guide is tested against the games it describes. We update content when games change or when better information becomes available.

Our Team

GameZone PK is run by a small editorial and development team led by Mujtaba Haider, an SEO and WordPress professional based in Pakistan. The team builds the games, writes the content, and maintains the site. You can reach us through the Contact Us page.

Why Trust Us

Because we are transparent. Our games are original, our reviews state their criteria, our privacy policy explains exactly what data is collected, and our advertising is clearly labelled. We earn money only through advertising after approval — we never charge players, and we never sell player data. If you ever find an error, tell us through the contact form and we will fix it.
